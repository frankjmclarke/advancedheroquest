What we actually learned

The two AHQ combat tables are arithmetic. Rulebook p43 reduces to:

hand-to-hand   hit = clamp(7 + defender_WS − attacker_WS, 2, 10)
ranged         hit = max(3, 12 − Bow_Skill + band)   Miss if raw > 12

Verified against all 144 + 60 printed cells, then independently against the seven Skaven cards in AHQ - Monsters 1.pdf. That's why both sheet sets compute their rows instead of transcribing them.

Two traps in there. The ranged row uses Current Bow Skill (after armour), only discoverable from the filled-in "Pierre Chancier" example on the sheets. And Toughness above 12 is not unwoundable — a damage die of 12 always wounds regardless of Toughness and re-rolls, so a T 18 Dreadnought gets chipped down on 12s. The 12 cap applies only to Heroes.

I got that second one wrong first. I initially flagged the T 14/16/18/20 enemies as broken and was going to rewrite them. The critical-damage rule vindicates them — but the Commander at T 14 was genuinely wrong, because he's a Hero, and Heroes cap at 12. Fixed to 8 base + 4 armour.

The engine's rules, from reading the source: Stairs_and_Chest is the keyword that makes a room a stairs-down candidate; the "Stairs Down" string is just printed text. Lair/Quest tables accept only None/Nothing/Chest/Stairs_and_Chest/Tomb as keywords, though Name() calls returning strings are unrestricted. Quests are listed by scanning tables/ at dialog-open, so a hand-written one just appears.

Verification that actually caught things. tabcheck.py only checks references, so I added throwaway checks for dice-range coverage, keyword legality against features.c, duplicate names, and call cycles — the range checker found a real pre-existing gap in ravenloft/treasure.tab and terror/treasure.tab (Magic-Potion jumps 1-2 → 5-7, so rolls of 3-4 fall through; still unfixed, not mine). A reachability check proved Deck 5 can only print Eldar. And every quest was loaded in the real hq_map.exe, which writes its title back to the profile on clean exit — that's the only externally observable proof a quest loaded.

Two things I fumbled. The Bash heredoc silently ate backslashes in one Python script and choked on another file, so larger content went through the Write tool instead. And a CRLF-blind regex made my first portable-zip test report the wrong quest — the run looked like a pass and wasn't.

I've saved three memories for next session: where the AHQ PDFs are and which have text, the hit-roll formulas with both traps, and the gh auth situation (git pushes work unattended, gh doesn't inherit it, and reading the stored credential is correctly blocked — so it needs ! gh auth login from you).
