The Trollslayer's Oath

This quest takes the Heroes into the ruined Dwarven colony of Durrag-Dol in the World's Edge Mountains. They are accompanied by the Trollslayer Kili Thekkrsson, who has sworn an oath to return the ancient warhammer Sognirstane, or "Roaring Stone," to the Temple of the Living Ancestor within the colony. Kili asks for no wages; his price is the Heroes' assistance in fulfilling the oath. He also forbids them to plunder Clan Durrag's ancestral treasures. Skaven property, on the other hand, is fair game, and the Imperial Dwarf League Council pays a bounty for Skaven ears.

Durrag-Dol is not simply another cave complex. It is a failed Dwarven settlement dating from the ancient Elf-Dwarf wars. The colony was never completed because its earl-king, hearth guard and warriors were wiped out while campaigning away from home. Without defenders, the surviving settlement was later overrun by Orcs and Goblins. In the present day its ruins have become a Skaven nest.

The scenario makes the physical structure of a Dwarven hold central to play. The upper portions consist of farm galleries, followed by the Main Shaft, then the Gauntlet, and ultimately the Kinhearth. Workshops and mines lie still deeper but are flooded and inaccessible. Kili's knowledge of traditional Dwarven construction is therefore crucial; unlike a normal dungeon where no one knows what lies ahead, the Heroes have a guide who understands the purpose and likely organization of the ancient spaces.

The opposition escalates as the party descends. Durrag-Dol is heavily infested by Skaven and their altered creatures, and the later levels contain powerful Skaven leaders. The special-character material identifies Shaffat as the nest's nominal Warlord but notes that Tsekkaz is the true dominant figure. Tsekkaz bears the Black Hand of Death, a warpstone-studded enchanted gauntlet capable of inflicting devastating damage on Humans, Dwarfs and Elves.

The goal is not fundamentally to loot or conquer the colony. The quest succeeds by carrying Sognirstane through the corrupted ruins and restoring it to its proper shrine. That makes Kili's oath the organizing principle of the entire adventure. The Heroes are visitors in a dead people's sacred home, and the restrictions on looting reinforce the distinction between ancestral property and the possessions of the Skaven occupiers.

The scenario thus combines a deep multi-level descent with a strong cultural theme: reclaiming, however briefly, a desecrated Dwarven hold so that an ancient obligation can finally be discharged.
