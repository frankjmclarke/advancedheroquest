Sentinel V

Sentinel V is the last of the Adeptus Mechanicus watch-stations strung along the Cadmus Reach, sunk into a dead moon and left to listen. For four hundred years it did. Six weeks ago it stopped in the middle of a routine sweep, and the sweep it was halfway through is still the last thing it transmitted.

A strike force teleports onto the upper spires. What they find on the way down is a sequence, not a siege: an Ork scavenger mob in the mooring clamps, its Nobz deeper in with Chaos Androids they did not build; the garrison gone into the coolant sumps and come back as a Genestealer brood; and on the command deck a warband of the Silent Hand, who opened the station and are the reason any of the rest got in.

None of them are why the Eldar are here. At the bottom of the shaft a warhost holds the Sentinel Core, and a Farseer is finishing something at the heart of the machine that was begun a long time before the Imperium put a station on this moon. They have opened a webway portal in the Core chamber. It is the objective, and it is also the only way off Sentinel V.

Five decks, played in order: Docking Spires (Gretchin and Orks), Cargo Holds (Orks, Nobz, Chaos Androids), Reactor Sumps (Genestealers, Androids, a Dreadnought), Command Spire (Chaos Space Marines and Dreadnoughts), and the Sentinel Core (Eldar only). Generate one map per deck; a deck ends at a Drop Shaft Down, the Core at the webway portal.
